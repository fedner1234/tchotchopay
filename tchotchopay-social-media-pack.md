### LinkedIn Post 2 — Industry Insight
**Caption:**
Haiti receives over $4 billion in remittances per year.

That's roughly 25% of the country's entire GDP.

Yet the average fee to send money to Haiti is still 7–10%.

For context: the UN's Sustainable Development Goal is to bring global remittance costs below 3%.

We're building **TchotchoPay** to close that gap — specifically for Haiti and the Haitian diaspora.

Our approach:
• USDC stablecoin on Base blockchain for near-instant, low-cost settlement
• Netlify-deployed platform with Reloadly integration for mobile top-ups
• Bridge (by Stripe) for USD ↔ USDC on/off-ramp infrastructure
• KYC/AML compliance built in from day one

We're pre-seed, building in public, and looking for:
→ Fintech investors who believe in emerging market financial infrastructure
→ Compliance and banking partners
→ Advisors with BaaS or remittance experience

If that's you — let's talk.
