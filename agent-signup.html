Tchotcho Pay Agent Agreement

As a Tchotcho Pay Cash Agent, you agree to: (1) Only process withdrawals for verified Tchotcho Pay users via the official Agent Dashboard. (2) Maintain your float securely and report any discrepancies within 24 hours. (3) Display Tchotcho Pay branding at your operating location. (4) Not charge customers additional fees beyond the platform rate. (5) Process requests within your stated operating hours. Agents earn 1% commission on all successfully processed transactions. Tchotcho Pay may suspend or terminate agent status for violations including but not limited to fraud, extended inactivity, or customer complaints.
