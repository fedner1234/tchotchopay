How It Works

Up and running in minutes

No bank account needed. Just a phone number and a government ID.

1
Download & Sign Up
Create your free account in under 3 minutes. Verify your identity with a government ID.
2
Add Funds
Load money via bank transfer (free), debit card (2%), or credit card (3%). Converts to USDC instantly.
3
Send or Pay
Send to anyone in Haiti by username, phone, or QR code. Pay bills, top up phones, save.
4
Cash Out Anytime
Recipients cash out HTG at 300+ agents across Haiti. Funds in hand in minutes.
