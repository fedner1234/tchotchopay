How It Works

Safe trades in 5 simple steps

TchotchoPay holds the USDC in escrow until both sides confirm. No one can run away.

1
🔍
Browse Offers
See live buy and sell offers from verified traders. Filter by payment method — MonCash, bank, or cash agent.
2
🔒
Escrow Locks USDC
When you accept a deal, TchotchoPay instantly locks the seller's USDC. It can't move until both sides confirm.
3
📱
Buyer Sends Payment
Buyer sends the agreed amount (e.g., 6,500 HTG) to the seller's MonCash or NatCash number outside the app.
4
📸
Upload Payment Proof
Buyer uploads a screenshot of the MonCash / NatCash SMS confirmation inside the TchotchoPay chat. All proof is stored on the trade record.
5
✅
Seller Confirms
Seller taps "I received the payment." If they don't confirm in 30 minutes after proof is uploaded, TchotchoPay opens an automatic dispute in the buyer's favor.
5
💎
USDC Released
TchotchoPay releases the locked USDC directly into the buyer's TchotchoPay wallet. Trade complete!
