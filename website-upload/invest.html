Technology

Enterprise Infrastructure. Startup Speed.

We're building on proven fintech rails — not reinventing the wheel. This lets us move fast, stay compliant, and scale without building a bank from scratch.

🌉
Stablecoin Layer

Bridge (by Stripe)

Powers our USDC wallets . Handles USD → USDC conversion, cross-border settlement, and stablecoin custody. Near-instant, low-cost, 24/7.

💳
Payments + Card Issuance

Stripe

Accepts debit, credit, and ACH deposits. Stripe Issuing powers our virtual and physical Visa Debit cards. One integration, two critical layers covered.

🏦
Bank Verification

Plaid

Instant bank account verification for ACH deposits. Users link their US bank account securely in minutes — no micro-deposit delays.

🛡️
Banking + Compliance

Unit / Synctera

Banking-as-a-Service layer providing FDIC-insured accounts, KYC/AML compliance, account ledgering, and regulatory coverage — no banking license required to launch.

⛓️
Secure Digital Network

Base (by Coinbase)

Layer 2 Ethereum network with ~$0.01 transaction fees and sub-second finality. USDC is the backbone of every transfer and wallet on Tchotcho Pay.

🇭🇹
Haiti Cash-Out Network

Local Partner + Agent Network

Partnership with MonCash (Digicel) for nationwide cash-out reach, supplemented by our own trained agent network in key Haitian cities.

Business Model

Five Revenue Streams. Built to Scale.

We make money when users transact — aligned incentives from day one.

Card Deposit Fee

2–3%

Charged when users load funds via debit or credit card. Standard for fintech deposit products. Highest volume stream.

Cash-Out Fee

1%

Charged when recipients withdraw to a local Haitian bank or through our agent network. Low friction, high frequency.

Merchant Fee

1–2%

Charged to marketplace merchants when customers pay with USDC. Standard interchange model — merchants pay, users don't.

FX Spread

~0.5%

Small spread on USD ↔ HTG conversions at cash-out. Transparent to users, meaningful at scale.
