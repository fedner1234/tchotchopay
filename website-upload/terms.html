2 Eligibility

To use Tchotcho Pay, you must:

  • Be at least 18 years of age

  • Have the legal capacity to enter into a binding contract

Tchotcho Pay currently serves users in the United States, Canada, France, and recipients in Haiti. Use of the Services from unauthorized jurisdictions is prohibited.

You must provide accurate, complete, and current information when creating your account. You are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of your PIN and account credentials.

To unlock full platform features, you must complete our Know Your Customer (KYC) identity verification process, which may require:

We may request additional information at any time to comply with applicable laws. Failure to provide requested information may result in account suspension or termination.

Never share your PIN or one-time passcode (OTP) with anyone, including Tchotcho Pay employees. We will never ask for your PIN.

4 Services Provided

Tchotcho Pay provides the following Services:

  • Money Transfer: Send USD-funded USDC to recipients in Haiti and other supported regions
  • USDC Wallet: Hold, send, and receive USDC (USD Coin)
  • Cash Out: Convert USDC to Haitian gourdes (HTG) via our agent network or local bank transfer
  • Marketplace: Purchase goods and services from verified Haitian merchants using USDC
  • P2P Payments: Send USDC to other Tchotcho Pay users by username, phone, or QR code

We reserve the right to modify, suspend, or discontinue any Service at any time with reasonable notice.

5 Fees & Charges

Tchotcho Pay charges fees for certain transactions. Current fees are:

  • Debit/Credit Card deposit: 2–3%
  • ACH / Bank transfer deposit: 0.5–1%
  • P2P transfers (user to user): Free
  • Cash out via agent or bank: 1%
  • Merchant processing fee: 1–2%
  • FX conversion spread (USD ↔ HTG): ~0.5–1%

All fees are disclosed before you confirm any transaction. We reserve the right to change our fee schedule with 30 days' written notice. Third-party fees (e.g., your card issuer's foreign transaction fees) are outside our control and are separate from Tchotcho Pay fees.

Violation of these prohibitions may result in immediate account suspension, fund freezes, and referral to law enforcement.

7 USDC & Digital Assets

Tchotcho Pay uses USDC (USD Coin) on the secure payment network for all internal transactions. USDC is a stablecoin pegged 1:1 to the US dollar and issued by Circle Internet Financial.

  • USDC balances in your Tchotcho Pay wallet are maintained
  • Tchotcho Pay does not lend, invest, or use customer USDC balances
  • Secure Digital Network transactions are irreversible once confirmed
  • Tchotcho Pay is not responsible for losses due to user error (e.g., sending to wrong address)
  • In the event of a payment network failure or congestion, we will make reasonable efforts to process your transaction but cannot guarantee timing
USDC is not a security, investment product, or bank deposit. It is a digital representation of one US dollar. It does not earn interest or returns unless explicitly stated.

Tchotcho Pay is a registered Money Services Business (MSB) with FinCEN. We take our anti-money laundering obligations seriously and are committed to preventing financial crime.

1 Our Regulatory Status

Tchotcho Pay LLC is registered with the Financial Crimes Enforcement Network (FinCEN) as a Money Services Business (MSB), FinCEN Registration Number: [pending]. We comply with:

  • Bank Secrecy Act (BSA) — U.S. federal AML law
  • USA PATRIOT Act — Customer identification and verification requirements
  • OFAC regulations — Sanctions screening requirements
  • State money transmission laws — Where applicable in operating states
  • FATF recommendations — International AML standards

2 Customer Identification Program (CIP)

As required by the USA PATRIOT Act, we collect and verify the identity of all customers before providing full account access. Our CIP requires:

  • Legal name
  • Date of birth
  • Residential address
  • Identification number (passport, national ID, SSN for U.S. persons)
  • Government-issued photo ID verification

Customers who do not complete verification are limited to reduced transaction thresholds and may not access all features.

3 Transaction Monitoring

We use automated and manual systems to monitor transactions for suspicious activity, including:

  • Large cash transactions: Transactions exceeding $10,000 (Currency Transaction Reports filed with FinCEN)
  • Structuring: Multiple transactions designed to avoid reporting thresholds
  • Rapid movement of funds: Large amounts sent and immediately withdrawn
  • Unusual geographic patterns: Transactions inconsistent with user profile
  • Sanctions hits: Names or addresses matching OFAC SDN or blocked person lists
  • PEP screening: Politically Exposed Person screening

Flagged transactions may be held for up to 72 hours for review. In cases of confirmed suspicious activity, we file a Suspicious Activity Report (SAR) with FinCEN.

Federal law prohibits us from notifying a customer that a SAR has been filed about them. If your transaction is held, it does not necessarily mean a SAR was filed.

4 Sanctions Compliance

Tchotcho Pay screens all users and transactions against:

  • OFAC Specially Designated Nationals (SDN) List
  • UN Security Council Consolidated List
  • EU Consolidated Financial Sanctions List
  • U.S. Commerce Department Entity List

We do not process transactions involving sanctioned countries, entities, or individuals. Currently sanctioned jurisdictions include (but are not limited to): Cuba, Iran, North Korea, Syria, Russia (certain sectors), and the Crimea/Donetsk/Luhansk regions.

Accounts that match sanctions lists will be frozen immediately and reported to OFAC.

5 Reporting Obligations

Tchotcho Pay is required to file the following reports with relevant authorities:

  • Currency Transaction Reports (CTRs): For cash transactions over $10,000
  • Suspicious Activity Reports (SARs): For suspected money laundering, fraud, or other financial crimes
  • FBAR (FinCEN 114): As applicable for foreign financial accounts

6 Agent & Merchant Compliance

All Tchotcho Pay agents and merchants are subject to enhanced due diligence:

  • Enhanced KYC verification including business registration documents
  • Signed AML compliance agreements
  • Ongoing transaction monitoring at the merchant/agent level
  • Regular compliance training provided by Tchotcho Pay
  • Immediate suspension for suspected AML violations

7 Record Keeping

In compliance with BSA requirements, Tchotcho Pay retains the following records for a minimum of 5 years:

  • Customer identification and KYC documents
  • All transaction records including sender, recipient, amount, date, and method
  • CTRs and SARs filed
  • All AML compliance training records
  • Risk assessment documentation

8 Report Suspicious Activity

If you suspect fraud, money laundering, or financial crime related to Tchotcho Pay — including activity by another user, merchant, or agent — please report it immediately:

  • Email: compliance@tchotchopay.com
  • In-app: Profile → Report an Issue → Suspicious Activity

All reports are confidential. Tchotcho Pay does not retaliate against good-faith reporters.
