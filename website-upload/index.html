Help Center

How can we help you?

How do I send money to someone in Haiti? +
Open the app, tap Send, search by username, phone, or QR. Enter amount, confirm with PIN. Recipient receives USDC instantly. Transfers between TchotchoPay users are always free.
How fast does the money arrive? +
Transfers between TchotchoPay wallets are instant — typically under 5 seconds. No bank processing delays.
What is USDC? +
A digital dollar. 1 USDC = $1. Issued by Circle, backed 1:1. Does not fluctuate like Bitcoin.
What are the fees? +
P2P free. Debit deposit 2%. Credit 3%. ACH free. Cash-out 1%. Bill pay and top-up free.
Can people in Haiti receive without a bank account? +
Yes. Recipients in Haiti receive directly to their TchotchoPay wallet, MonCash, or NatCash. No bank account required. They can cash out through 300+ agents nationwide.
Is TchotchoPay safe and regulated? +
Yes. We are registered as a Money Services Business with FinCEN. We use bank-grade AES-256 encryption, KYC via Persona, and USDC reserves held 1:1 with Circle.
