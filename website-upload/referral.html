Questions

Common questions

When do I get my $10 reward? ▾
Your $10 USD lands in your TchotchoPay wallet within 24 hours of your friend completing their first transfer. You'll get a push notification when it arrives.
Is there a limit on how many friends I can refer? ▾
No limit. Refer 100 friends, earn $1,000. The more you share, the more you earn. We've had users earn over $500 in a single month.
What counts as a "first transfer"? ▾
Any transfer of at least $10 to Haiti within 30 days of signing up qualifies. Top-ups, bill payments, and wallet-to-wallet transfers all count.
Can I cash out my referral earnings? ▾
Yes! Referral earnings are credited as USD to your wallet. You can use them to send money to Haiti, top up phones, or withdraw to your bank account.
Can I refer people who already have a TchotchoPay account? ▾
No — the referral bonus only applies to new users who sign up using your unique link. Existing accounts are not eligible.
