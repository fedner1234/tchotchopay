## PRODUCTION NOTES

**Music:** Upbeat, light — no lyrics. Search "motivational upbeat background music" on CapCut or Canva.

**Colors:**
- Background: #020B2D (navy) or #0A1F6E (dark blue)
- Accent text: #00AEEF (cyan) and #00C896 (green)
- White for main body text

**Fonts:** Bold sans-serif for headlines. Clean, readable at small sizes.

**Stock footage ideas (free on Pexels / Pixabay):**
- Person smiling at phone
- Haiti street scene or family
- Phone screen close-up
- Money transfer / digital payment

**Canva tip:** Search "mobile money transfer reels template" — customize with TchotchoPay colors and swap placeholder text with this script.

**CapCut tip:** Use "Auto Captions" to generate subtitles automatically from voiceover.
