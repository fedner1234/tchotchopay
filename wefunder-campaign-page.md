## The Problem

Haiti is one of the most remittance-dependent countries on earth. Remittances make up **~25% of Haiti's entire GDP** — more than foreign aid, more than exports. Families rely on money sent from New York, Miami, Montreal, and Paris to survive.

Yet the system is broken in three critical ways:

**1. It's expensive.** Western Union and MoneyGram charge 5–8% per transfer. On a $200 transfer, that's $10–16 gone before the family sees a dollar.

**2. It's slow.** Bank transfers take 1–3 business days. Cash pickup requires the recipient to physically travel to an agent location — often unsafe, often far.

**3. The money loses value instantly.** Haiti's gourde (HTG) has lost over 80% of its value in the past 5 years. The moment a family converts USD to HTG, inflation starts eating it.

There is no modern, digital-first solution built specifically for this community. We're building it.

## How It Works

**Step 1 — Load money**
The sender in the U.S. loads funds via debit card, ACH bank transfer, or credit card.

**Step 2 — Convert to USDC**
TchotchoPay automatically converts USD to USDC on the Base blockchain. The recipient's wallet is credited in seconds.

**Step 3 — Recipient chooses**
Marie in Port-au-Prince receives a notification. She can:
- Keep it as USDC savings
- Pay her electricity bill directly in the app
- Send $20 to her sister across town
- Cash out at a local agent in HTG

**Step 4 — No bank required**
The entire flow works on a smartphone. No bank account needed. No Western Union line. No travel.

## Business Model

TchotchoPay earns revenue four ways:

1. **Card deposit fee** — 2–3% when senders load with debit or credit card
2. **Cash-out fee** — 1% when recipients withdraw to a bank or agent
3. **Merchant fee** — 1–2% on marketplace purchases
4. **FX spread** — small margin when converting between USD and HTG

Peer-to-peer transfers inside TchotchoPay are **always free** — that's how we build habit and loyalty.

Premium accounts (higher limits, faster withdrawals, business tools) will be an additional revenue stream as we grow.

## FAQ

**Is this a crypto company?**
We use blockchain infrastructure (Base/USDC) on the backend for speed and low cost — but users see a simple dollar wallet, not crypto. You don't need to understand blockchain to use TchotchoPay.

**What about regulation?**
We are registering as a Money Services Business (MSB) with FinCEN and pursuing state money transmitter licenses. We have already incorporated in both the U.S. (Delaware C-Corp) and Haiti (TchotchoPay, S.A.) and are preparing our BRH application.

**Who are your competitors?**
Western Union, MoneyGram, and CAM Transfer dominate remittances to Haiti but charge high fees and are slow. MonCash (Digicel) operates in Haiti but has no U.S.-side product for the diaspora. No competitor combines the sender-side U.S. loading, USDC wallet, marketplace, and cash-out in one product.

**When will the app launch?**
We plan to launch a beta with our first users within 6–9 months of closing this round.
