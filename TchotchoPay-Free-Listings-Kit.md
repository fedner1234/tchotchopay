### Full Description
TchotchoPay is a next-generation digital payment platform built for the Haitian diaspora. We make it fast, affordable, and simple to send money to Haiti from the U.S., Canada, France, and beyond.

Unlike traditional remittance services that charge 5–10% in fees and take days to deliver funds, TchotchoPay delivers money in minutes directly to MonCash and NatCash wallets — the most widely used mobile wallets in Haiti — at a fraction of the cost.

Every TchotchoPay user gets a USDC digital wallet for instant transfers, dollar savings protected from inflation, and access to a Haitian marketplace where recipients can pay bills, buy phone top-ups, pay school fees, and send to family — all without converting back to cash.

Key features:
- Send to MonCash & NatCash wallets in minutes
- USDC digital dollar savings account
- Peer-to-peer payments by username, phone, or QR code
- Haitian marketplace — bills, top-ups, local merchants
- Visa Virtual Debit Card
- Mobile top-up for Haiti, Dominican Republic, Cuba & more
- KYC/AML compliance via Persona
- Bank-link via Plaid
- Card processing via Stripe
- Card issuing via Lithic

TchotchoPay is building the financial infrastructure Haiti's diaspora deserves — combining the best of MonCash, Cash App, and Venmo into one platform rooted in Haitian culture.
