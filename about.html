Our Story

Why we built this

Every month, millions of Haitians abroad send money home. They wait in line at Western Union. They pay $7 on a $100 transfer. They watch exchange rates that disappear into someone else's margin.

We grew up watching our families do this. We've done it ourselves. The diaspora sends over $3.7 billion to Haiti every year — and somewhere between 8% and 12% of every dollar evaporates before it reaches a family member.

Tchotcho Pay was born from a simple belief: the people who need money the most should lose the least of it in transit. With USDC on Base blockchain, we can move dollars in minutes for fractions of a cent.

We're not just a remittance app. We're building Haiti's financial infrastructure — a wallet, a marketplace, a savings account, and a payment network, all in one.

Roadmap

How we got here & where we're going

Q1 2025
Company Founded
Tchotcho Pay LLC incorporated. Core team assembled. Initial research into Haiti remittance corridors and USDC infrastructure.
Q2 2025
Product Architecture
USDC on Base selected as core settlement layer. Smart contract architecture designed. Compliance framework established with legal counsel.
Q3 2025
Beta Development
App prototype built. 50 beta users onboarded from the Haitian diaspora in New York, Miami, and Boston. First real transfer tested.
Q4 2025 — Q1 2026
Waitlist & Community Launch
Public waitlist opened. 2,000+ signups in first 30 days. Marketplace merchant pilot in Port-au-Prince with 12 businesses. Mobile top-up launched.
Q3 2026 — Target Launch
Full Public Launch
App available on iOS & Android. Full marketplace live. Agent network across Haiti. Bill pay (EDH, school fees). USDC savings accounts.
2027
Expand to Caribbean & Africa
Replicate the model in Jamaica, Dominican Republic, and French-speaking West Africa. Build the pan-Caribbean USDC payment network.
