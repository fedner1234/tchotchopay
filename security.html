Digital Dollar

Why USDC? Why not regular dollars?

USDC lets us move money faster, cheaper, and more transparently than any traditional bank.

💵 What is USDC?

USDC (USD Coin) is a digital dollar created by Circle, the company co-founded by Coinbase. It is fully regulated by US financial authorities and audited monthly by a major accounting firm (Grant Thornton).

For every 1 USDC in circulation, there is exactly $1 held in a US bank or US Treasury — verified publicly every month. It is not a volatile cryptocurrency like Bitcoin.

⛓️ What is Base blockchain?

Base is a blockchain network built by Coinbase — one of the largest and most trusted crypto companies in the world, publicly traded on NASDAQ.

Base lets us settle transfers in ~2 seconds for a fraction of a cent — compared to 2–5 days and $25+ fees for international bank wires. Every transaction is permanently recorded and publicly verifiable.

✅
The bottom line
Your USDC balance is always equal to US dollars. It cannot lose value due to inflation or devaluation. Holding USDC is safer than holding Haitian gourdes, which lose ~20% of their value per year.
